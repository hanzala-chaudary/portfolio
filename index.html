About Me

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore, with an expected graduation in 2027. My academic background includes chemical process design, thermodynamics, heat and mass transfer, fluid mechanics, and reaction engineering. I'm particularly interested in understanding how engineering principles translate into practical industrial applications.

During my internship at Packages Convertors Limited, I gained exposure to industrial production workflows, multilayer lamination processes, and quality control practices. Beyond industrial training, I've worked on academic projects involving heat exchanger design, equipment sizing, process calculations, and sustainable soap manufacturing. I aim to further develop my technical and analytical skills to pursue opportunities in process engineering, manufacturing, and industrial operations.
